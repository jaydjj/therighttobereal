March 8 · The Beauty Blackout What's this about?

Our insecurities are someone else's business model.

The idea

We're happy to serve you. Not sell to you.

A whole industry runs on making people feel like they're not quite enough. Too old, too soft, too lined, too much, not enough. Then it sells the fix.

The Right to Be Real is a movement against that pressure. Everyone deserves to decide how they look, who they are and what they're worth, without unrealistic expectations or insecurity made to order.

We're not here to tell anyone how to look, live or identify. Wear the makeup, skip the makeup, do whatever you like. We're here to question the forces that make us feel we have to change to be worthy.

House rules

How we do things around here.

  • Blame the system, not the person. We challenge expectations and industries, never someone's individual choices.
  • No one tells you what "real" looks like. Not even us. Diversity is the point.
  • Everyone's welcome. Insecurity affects people of every gender and identity.
  • No shame on the menu. We trade in autonomy, empathy and critical thinking.
